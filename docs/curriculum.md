# Curriculum (TEMP EXPERIMENTAL)

## Overview

- **Goal:** Teach English speakers the Japanese they need to get around while traveling in Japan.
- **Level:** Beginner, roughly CEFR / JF Standard A1.
- **Syllabus type:** Situational (task-based) syllabus for survival Japanese.
- **Reference model:** The Japan Foundation's *Marugoto* course and its can-do statements (e.g. "I can order food at a restaurant").

## Structure

Content is organized by **situation**. Each unit covers one real travel situation, with its vocabulary, phrases, and listening practice nested inside it.

Each unit should define:

- **Can-do goals:** what the learner can do after the unit
- **Vocabulary:** words for the situation
- **Phrases:** what the learner says
- **Listening:** what the learner will hear from staff or locals
- **Culture notes:** relevant etiquette

## Language Components

These run across all units.

### Scripts

- Hiragana
- Katakana (high value: menus and loanwords)
- Survival kanji for signs: 出口, 入口, 駅, 男, 女, 禁煙, 営業中, 準備中

### Numbers & Counters

- 〜つ, 〜人, 〜枚, 〜本, 〜個
- Prices in 円
- Times and dates

### Grammar Patterns

| Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 〜をください | Please give me ~ |
| 〜はどこですか | Where is ~? |
| 〜ありますか | Do you have ~? |
| 〜てもいいですか | May I ~? |
| 〜たいです | I want to ~ |
| 〜でお願いします | ~, please (choosing an option) |

### Pronunciation

- Long vowels
- Small っ
- Mora timing
- Basic pitch accent

### Receptive Keigo

Understanding what staff say, e.g. いらっしゃいませ, 少々お待ちください, 〜でよろしいですか.

This is the most commonly missed piece: learners can ask questions but can't parse the reply.

## Communication Strategies

Phrases for when communication breaks down. Beginners rely on these constantly.

| Phrase | Use |
|---|---|
| もう一度お願いします | Please say it again |
| ゆっくりお願いします | Please speak slowly |
| わかりません | I don't understand |
| 英語大丈夫ですか | Is English OK? |
| これ (plus pointing) | This one |
| 大丈夫です | No thanks (polite decline) |

## Culture & Pragmatics

- No tipping
- Shoes off indoors
- Quiet on trains
- Trash handling
- Onsen rules
- Queueing
- Handing money on the tray

## Skills

Every unit trains **reading, listening, and speaking**.

- **Listening** is weighted heaviest, since understanding the response is the hardest part of real travel.
- **Writing** is optional for this goal.
